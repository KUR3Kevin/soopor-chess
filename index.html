Classic, Super, PvP, CvP, and perks

Game Modes

Classic is standard chess. Super reveals the perk system.

PvP is player-vs-player. CvP lets you play against the computer.

Perks

  • Pawn Sprint — once per game, a pawn may move four squares on its first move if the path is clear.
  • Double Turn — take two moves in one go, using any two pieces except the king and queen.
  • Pawn Recall — when a pawn reaches the far side, spawn a pawn in the middle and bring back one captured piece.
  • Triple Advance — setup phase only: before your first regular move, send one pawn three squares forward.

How they work

Each perk is a one-time rule per side. Once used, it stays spent for the rest of the game.

Phone Tips

On small screens, the board stacks above the side rail. Open SUPER to slide the perk drawer into view.
